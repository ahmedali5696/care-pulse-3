## Permanent scalability rules for Wave 4, Wave 5 and future Masters

The Product Owner approved these continuing CI engineering rules on 27 November 2026. They apply when an authorized Master is planned, implemented and reviewed, including Wave 4 and Wave 5. They supplement the [Constitution](../.specify/memory/constitution.md) and [approved implementation plan](implementation-plan.md#3-agent-execution-rules-and-testing-strategy); neither the rules nor dependency readiness authorize execution of a future Master, modification of an approved authority, or release.

Retain the current five-lane Full Foundation architecture: `ordinary-db-core`, `ordinary-db-clinical`, `ordinary-stack`, `historical` and `compat`, with the existing common checks and final `Full Foundation acceptance` gate. The Phase 2 executable candidate and acceptance record below are the current baseline, not a new production performance target.

1. **Dependency-aware test isolation.** Assign a suite according to its actual database, schema, fixture, service, browser and ordering dependencies. Prefer self-contained deterministic tenant fixtures and isolate independent suites from unrelated accumulated data. Preserve required shared state, ordered transaction checkpoints, signed identity and cross-operation sequences within their owning proof; never split a scenario so that its dependency or observable effects disappear. Keep unrestricted RLS visibility probes, actual cross-tenant fixtures, real PostgreSQL, migration and cleanup proofs. Retain the immutable Historical and bounded Compatibility selections and protected diagnostic modes.

2. **Measure after each Master.** As part of each Master's existing integration evidence, record per-suite elapsed times, Full workflow elapsed, common/lane/gate durations, the actual critical path and aggregate unrounded runner-minutes. Record the tested SHA, run and attempt, inventory/schema, relevant pinned inputs and observed runner environment; compare against the preceding accepted baseline and identify meaningful growth or imbalance. Distinguish changes in coverage or environment from like-for-like comparisons. Use the already required acceptance run and its logs where available; missing measurement is an evidence limitation, not grounds to claim an unmeasured improvement or to dispatch extra CI without authorization. Timings inform engineering review, not a numerical pass/fail gate.

3. **Evidence-based lane assignment and adaptive partitioning.** Place new suites in the existing lane that satisfies their dependencies and measured execution characteristics, not merely the lane with the fewest cases or the same Master label. Keep service/browser-dependent proofs in Stack and preserve historical/compatibility provenance. Reassess the distribution when measured timings or dependency changes justify it; prefer minimal reordering or reassignment within the five lanes. A proposed change must document its dependency analysis, measured bottleneck, expected critical-path and aggregate-cost impact, and exact selector/report parity, with the controller, inventory, receipt validation, regression checks and documentation reconciled under separate authorization. Do not repartition automatically after each Master or add lanes merely because the inventory grows.

4. **Reuse verified integration evidence without weakening coverage.** Map each applicable invariant, acceptance criterion and concurrency obligation to its owning verified test/report and exact execution evidence. Reuse that evidence in later review and integrated assurance rather than duplicating an identical proof when it actually establishes the same requirement, actors, boundary, conditions and effects. For D-12/B-27, retain all approved integrated database, API, worker, file, security and concurrency requirements, including distinct cross-operation proofs, real races and rollback/zero-effect assertions; add focused integrated coverage for gaps, changed behavior or interactions not proved by owner-task evidence. U-12 retains its approved representative UI and acceptance mapping. Reuse of evidence is traceability, not reuse of test execution: all mandatory selectors, intentional repeated scenarios, assertions, case identities and reports remain required in the reviewed current inventory. A prior SHA's pass, cached result, focused diagnostic, profiling run or Fast Feedback cannot substitute for current exact-SHA Full acceptance; never mix receipts across runs or attempts. No existing required test or proof is removed by these rules.

5. **Preserve minimal Playwright E2E scope.** Follow implementation plan section 3 and U-12, including the approved narrow first-owner invite/login, patient-to-book-to-check-in-to-admit-to-complete journey, Emergency/Main independence, authorized denial, Arabic RTL, archive/export and one critical reschedule path, together with U-12's specified representative follow-up, atomic Urgent/bulk negative path, old-day review, Doctor correction, secure file, two-tenant and accessibility/locale/responsive verification. Keep the existing required browser/BFF scenarios and Wave 5 build/typecheck/lint gates. Prove critical races, field permissions and detailed business/security combinations at their authoritative API/DB boundaries; do not duplicate all concurrency cases or introduce exhaustive UI permutations, another browser framework or new test infrastructure. The approved plan remains the authority for the exact representative journeys and applicable acceptance coverage.

6. **Investigate bottlenecks before adding lanes.** Use actual per-suite timings to isolate setup, execution, teardown and relevant database/service cost; inspect dependency ordering, fixture accumulation and sanitized evidence before choosing a correction. Distinguish proven causes from hypotheses. Prefer the smallest dependency-safe isolation, ordering or assignment correction; compare elapsed/critical-path/aggregate runner time and verify unchanged coverage and correctness when authorized. Additional lanes are not authorized by this policy: investigate and demonstrate the need first, then seek separate PO approval. Do not weaken security, narrow assertions, alter published migrations, mask failures with retries or timeout increases, optimize production RLS speculatively, or add infrastructure to solve a CI-only observation.

All existing Full Foundation requirements remain binding: pinned inputs, real isolated environments, current schema and approved historical/compatibility provenance, exact reviewed report counts and case-identity digests, zero mandatory failures/skips, scoped cleanup, strict artifact digest and same-repository/workflow/SHA/run/attempt validation, and the unchanged final check identity. The 15-20-minute Phase 2 objective is historical engineering context, not a mandatory threshold for any future Master or Wave. No rule overrides the approved task prerequisites, security/domain invariants, release gates or PO authorization boundaries.
