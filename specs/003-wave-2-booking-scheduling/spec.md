### Session 2026-10-28

Historical October clarification record: the answers below are preserved verbatim. November requirements below supersede conflicting role, decision and extension assumptions. The first ten approved answers are preserved from the earlier sessions. The final session adds only CHK002/CHK011, each explicitly accepted as Option A by the Product Owner.

- Q: Should new bookings reject past Cairo service dates and timed intervals whose start has already passed today? (CHK001) → A: Reject both. This Product Owner clarification adds no minimum advance duration for future starts.
- Q: Must a timed booking exactly match a configured slot, or may staff book any otherwise valid interval? (CHK021) → A: Match a current valid manual or unsuppressed generated slot exactly; all date, interval and capacity guards still apply.
- Q: Must staff acknowledge the New Examination type and ordinary price before a nonqualifying Follow-up fallback commits? (CHK035) → A: Yes; explicit acknowledgment of the actual type and price is required before commit. Wave 2 supplies the service contract, not a UI.
- Q: What happens to Follow-up discount inputs when staff confirm a New Examination fallback? (CHK036) → A: Staff explicitly choose or reaffirm the discount, including NONE, and any required reason against the ordinary base before acknowledging the final price. Preserve inputs for review; neither silently carry them over nor silently discard them.
- Q: At what level is Open versus Exact-Time chosen, and may new bookings use both modes on one date? (CHK003) → A: One clinic-wide active mode for new bookings, with no date/period mode override or per-booking choice. Preserve previously confirmed bookings; records from before and after a mode change may coexist on a date without converting either.
- Q: Which capacity rule should apply when the Owner enables overlapping Exact-Time bookings? (CHK006) → A: Use saved examination capacity directly. Enabling overlap requires explicitly confirming its current integer value, at least 2; no separate booking limit or additional ceiling. Capacity-changing controls remain in Wave 3.
- Q: How should duration-generated booking slots behave around breaks and leftover time at the end of a working period? (CHK007) → A: Generate from each period's start at duration-sized steps. Omit slots intersecting breaks; do not restart after breaks. Discard trailing time shorter than a full slot.
- Q: Should manually configured booking slots repeat weekly, apply only to specific dates, or support both? (CHK008) → A: Both: weekly slots normally apply; an explicitly configured date-specific manual set replaces them for that date. All applicable work-period, break, holiday, capacity and affected-booking review guards remain.
- Q: What deadline limits should apply when a Doctor records a justified exception to the normal seven-day Follow-up window? (CHK013) → A: Deadline on/after the source completion date; may shorten below day seven or extend beyond it, with no maximum duration. Each exception requires a specific deadline and recorded Doctor justification. Confirmed claims retain their preserved entitlement; their extensions remain separate, prior Doctor-audited authorizations.
- Q: May preferential Follow-up be booked for the source examination's completion day, or must it wait until the next calendar day? (CHK014) → A: Completion day is eligible; dates before completion are never eligible. All ordinary booking guards remain. A source Appointment whose original service date is today still blocks another counted appointment today; an older original service date does not itself occupy today's patient/day allowance.
- Q: How far into the future should staff be allowed to create a new booking? (CHK002) → A: No maximum booking horizon in Wave 2; all other eligibility guards remain, including the authorized preferential Follow-up deadline.
- Q: What fractional precision should staff be allowed to enter for a percentage discount? (CHK011) → A: No additional percentage fractional-digit cap; retain the full accepted finite decimal value in [0,100], subject to existing request-size safeguards. Calculate exactly without intermediate rounding; round only the final net to two EGP fractional digits.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

Story priorities express business value, not new execution dependencies. Each story can be verified with its listed prerequisites and valid synthetic records; a foundation scenario does not authorize the later operational workflow it supports.

### User Story 1 - Book a Routine New Examination reliably (Priority: P1)

As a Doctor/Owner or Receptionist, I can select an existing active patient and confirm an eligible day-only or configured timed New Examination with a reliable booking number and frozen service value.

**Why this priority**: Ordinary booking is the core Wave 2 outcome and must not imply attendance, payment or clinical care.

**Independent Test**: With authorized staff, separate patient identities and configured prices, confirm bookings and inspect the saved date, number, type, price and history. Exercise competing submissions and denied requests without performing check-in or admission.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an active patient, an eligible date and a valid price, **When** either authorized staff role books an Open New Examination, **Then** one Routine Appointment is confirmed as Booked with a unique clinic/day number, frozen EGP snapshot and history, without an invented time, Waiting entry or Visit.
2. **Given** two patients sharing a family phone, **When** staff book the selected patient, **Then** only that patient's identity/history is linked; shared demographics do not combine bookings or histories.
3. **Given** a counted same-patient/same-day Appointment in Booked, Waiting, In Examination or Completed, **When** another booking competes, **Then** at most one counted Appointment exists. Cancelled or No-show history permits a distinct new ID and number, without reopening the old record or reusing its number.
4. **Given** a successful booking, **When** the same authorized request is retried with the same operation identity and content, **Then** the original result is returned once; changed content conflicts, and revoked access cannot retrieve a cached result. If the response was lost, recovery determines the committed result before another mutation is attempted.
5. **Given** archive and new booking compete, **When** archive commits first, **Then** new-ID creation fails without partial records. If booking commits first, later archive preserves that confirmed Appointment and its downstream existing-ID continuity.
6. **Given** a foreign-clinic ID, disabled user, suspended clinic, forged status/price/number, or invalid date/interval, **When** booking is attempted, **Then** it is denied without disclosure or successful booking, number assignment, snapshot, claim, history or notification effects.
7. **Given** Routine walk-in is OFF or the Doctor is paused, **When** a valid ordinary booking is requested, **Then** it still creates Booked only; no combined booking-and-attendance shortcut bypasses the walk-in policy and no arbitrary minimum advance-booking time is imposed.
8. **Given** a new ordinary or preferential booking, **When** its service date is before today's Cairo date or its timed start has already passed today, **Then** confirmation is rejected without booking effects. Future timed starts have no additional minimum lead time; this rule does not alter previously confirmed records or later-wave existing-ID workflows.
9. **Given** otherwise eligible Open dates or configured Exact-Time candidates 366 and 3,650 Cairo calendar days ahead, **When** staff request availability and confirm a new booking, **Then** neither operation rejects solely because of distance into the future; Wave 2 imposes no maximum horizon. These are synthetic examples, not thresholds. Calendar, slot, capacity, price, authorization, patient/day and archive guards remain; preferential Follow-up still requires its authorized deadline, with any nonqualifying fallback following US4.8.

### User Story 2 - Configure prices and preserve confirmed value (Priority: P1)

As the Owner or a Receptionist with a live operational_manager grant, I can configure standard, preferential Follow-up and eligible fixed Urgent pricing. As authorized booking staff, I can apply a permitted discount or non-Urgent free designation with its reason, knowing that later settings cannot rewrite a confirmed value.

**Why this priority**: Reliable prices and authorization are prerequisites for booking and future Urgent intake; a service value must never be mistaken for payment received.

**Independent Test**: Configure and revise prices as Owner and delegated Manager, deny ordinary/revoked Receptionists, and compare confirmed snapshots before and after edits. Test Urgent validation without introducing an Urgent intake workflow.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** Owner authority or a live Owner-granted operational_manager capability, **When** valid pricing/Follow-up settings are saved, **Then** a new policy version records the actual actor/role and immutable audit; ordinary, disabled, revoked, stale-grant or foreign Receptionists and operators fail. Revocation also denies receipt recovery. Unset prices remain unset until configured; Manager cannot manage accounts or capacity.
2. **Given** a 100.00 EGP base, **When** staff apply a reasoned 10.00 EGP fixed discount or 10 percent discount, **Then** net is 90.00 EGP. A valid non-Urgent free designation gives 0.00 EGP; negative discounts, above-base discounts, non-finite values, forged totals and missing discount reasons fail.
3. **Given** a 10.05 EGP base and 50 percent non-Urgent discount, **When** net is calculated, **Then** the exact intermediate 5.025 becomes 5.03 EGP, rounded once at the final net, with two fractional digits.
4. **Given** standard base 100.00 EGP, **When** Urgent is configured/enabled, **Then** its fixed base must exceed 100.00 and valid operating periods must exist; any proposed discounted Urgent rounded net at or below 100.00, or Free Urgent, fails. Urgent remains independently OFF by default.
5. **Given** enabled Urgent base 150.00 EGP, **When** Owner proposes standard base 150.00 or higher, **Then** the edit is rejected unless Urgent is expressly corrected or disabled. Competing policy changes and price confirmation observe one complete valid policy version, never mixed values.
6. **Given** a confirmed ordinary or preferential booking, **When** future prices or Follow-up policy change, **Then** its price, discount evidence and confirmed entitlement remain unchanged. Emergency pricing foundations use standard New Examination value, not the Urgent premium; neither label records payment.
7. **Given** a reasoned percentage discount, **When** its finite decimal value is in [0,100] and fits existing request safeguards, **Then** no fractional-digit cap is imposed: 0%, 100%, 12.345% and longer fractional values remain subject to the same service guards. On a 100.00 EGP non-Urgent base, 12.345% gives 87.66 EGP; on a 1.00 EGP base, 0.500000000000000000000000000001% gives 0.99 EGP, not 1.00 from premature precision loss. Preserve the full accepted percentage value in quote/snapshot evidence, without rounding it to EGP precision; negative, above-100 or nonfinite percentages fail atomically. Existing reason, Free and Urgent premium guards remain.

### User Story 3 - Publish a calendar and offer valid exact times (Priority: P1)

As the Owner or a Receptionist with a live operational_manager grant, I can configure working days, optional operating periods, breaks, holidays and date overrides, and preview affected confirmed bookings before activating a change. Staff can offer only valid timed candidates when Exact-Time is configured.

**Why this priority**: Booking availability must reflect the actual service calendar without silently invalidating prior commitments or inventing operating hours.

**Independent Test**: Set up representative dates, periods, manual slots and generated slots, confirm Open/timed bookings, and attempt schedule activation and concurrent conflicting bookings against those records.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** initial clinic defaults, **When** calendar settings are inspected, **Then** booking is Open, there are no inferred hours/duration/closing time, breaks or automatic cutoff, overlap is OFF and holiday/non-working-day booking is OFF. Open booking has no hidden time-derived daily cap.
2. **Given** a holiday/non-working date, **When** ordinary booking is attempted, **Then** OFF denies both Open and Exact-Time. ON permits eligible Open without invented hours; Exact-Time additionally needs a positive explicitly applicable date-specific opening period, outside breaks. A holiday toggle alone supplies no operating period and never permits Paid Urgent on a holiday.
3. **Given** weekly periods and an explicit valid date override, **When** that date's availability is evaluated, **Then** the override governs; a closed holiday cannot borrow weekly hours as an implicit opening. Invalid/nonpositive periods or invalid break/slot placement are rejected.
4. **Given** manual and duration-generated candidates coexist, **When** candidates are offered, **Then** generation starts at each applicable working period's start and advances in positive duration-sized steps, retaining only full intervals ending within that period and outside breaks. Break-intersecting slots are omitted without restarting the grid; trailing remainders are discarded. Every generated candidate intersecting a valid manual one is then suppressed. Manual intervals still obey date, work-period and break guards. For a synthetic 09:00–11:20 period, 30-minute duration and 09:40–09:50 break, generated slots before manual suppression are 09:00–09:30, 10:00–10:30 and 10:30–11:00; neither a 09:50 restart nor a short 11:00–11:20 slot is offered.
5. **Given** timed intervals 09:00–09:30 and 09:30–10:00 within a valid period, **When** booked, **Then** touching endpoints are allowed. Intersecting intervals fail by default; expressly enabled Exact-Time overlap uses saved examination capacity directly, with no separate booking limit or additional ceiling. Enabling requires configured Exact-Time slots and explicit confirmation of the current integer capacity of at least 2; missing confirmation, stale capacity evidence or capacity 1 fails. Confirmation rechecks current saved capacity, and competing bookings cannot oversubscribe it. This neither changes examination capacity nor supplies admission approval.
6. **Given** a Cairo daylight-saving transition, **When** an entered local time is nonexistent or repeated without disambiguation, **Then** it is rejected; an explicit offset or instant uniquely resolving a valid local time may be accepted. The intended Cairo service date is retained and interval comparison is unambiguous.
7. **Given** proposed periods, breaks, holidays, booking mode, manual slots, generated duration, overlap/effective timed-limit or cutoff changes affect confirmed bookings, **When** an authorized settings actor previews the change, **Then** affected Open and Exact-Time bookings and conflicts are identified. A proposed effective timed limit below existing peak occupancy is a conflict, including turning overlap OFF while overlapping bookings remain. The effective limit is 1 with overlap OFF and current saved examination capacity with overlap ON; schedule changes never edit that saved capacity. Activation fails until conflicts are explicitly resolved by authorized cancellation/rescheduling; it never performs those actions implicitly or reprices history. Candidate changes never rewrite existing intervals or retroactively require them to match newly generated candidates. Per the approved Product Owner decision (2026-10-28), proposed date/operating-period/break invalidation blocks activation for future `Booked` and `Waiting`, including dates with no `Booked`; `In Examination` and `Completed` retain ongoing/completed care and are not retroactively disqualified by those changes. All four counted timed statuses still contribute to peak occupancy on future dates, even without `Booked` records or a change to overlap/capacity settings; a proposed effective limit below the actual peak blocks activation. Cutoff changes show prospective reconciliation exposure for still-Booked records, not an immediate state transition or an invented closing-time conflict.
8. **Given** a reviewed preview and a newly confirmed conflicting booking or changed relevant revision, **When** activation is attempted, **Then** fresh affected-booking validation rejects the stale change and requires renewed review/resolution. Booking and activation cannot both commit contradictory outcomes.
9. **Given** no automatic cutoff, **When** an authorized settings actor enables one without specifying a valid Cairo-local cutoff time, **Then** configuration fails; an explicitly specified valid time may be saved separately from work-period end. This configures future reconciliation, not a Wave 2 No-show transition or scheduler.
10. **Given** a new timed booking, **When** its endpoints do not exactly match a current valid manual or unsuppressed generated candidate, **Then** it is rejected even if the interval otherwise fits working hours and capacity; confirmation rechecks candidate membership as well as availability.
11. **Given** a clinic-wide booking-mode change, **When** it is previewed and activated, **Then** existing Open bookings keep null intervals and existing timed bookings keep their original intervals and snapshots; only new bookings use the active mode. Mode and slot changes are included in the affected-booking review; no interval is invented or erased and no booking is silently cancelled or repriced.
12. **Given** weekly manual slots and an explicitly configured date-specific manual set, **When** that date's candidates are resolved, **Then** the date-specific set replaces the weekly manual set, rather than merging with it; without a date-specific set, use the weekly set for that weekday. This selects manual candidates only: applicable date/work/break/holiday guards still bind, generation keeps its approved period-start grid, and only valid effective manual candidates suppress generated intersections. Changes follow affected-booking preview without rewriting existing confirmed intervals.

### User Story 4 - Reserve one justified preferential Follow-up (Priority: P1)

As authorized clinic staff, I can record an actual-actor source decision, revise an earlier Receptionist-authored decision within Doctor precedence, reserve one qualifying reduced/free Follow-up, authorize a specific NEW-booking date exception, or separately extend a confirmed claim with the required evidence. These actions preserve frozen source/price and Doctor clinical authorship.

**Why this priority**: Preferential care is an approved booking type with source, date, provenance and single-use safeguards.

**Independent Test**: Use synthetic Completed Routine/Emergency/Urgent New Examination Visits, including actual-actor administrative completion and no source decision. Verify all staff variants, reason/date rules, Doctor precedence and races without implementing admission or completion commands.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a qualifying Completed New Examination for the same patient, Doctor and clinic, including valid administrative completion and no Doctor decision, **When** staff confirm a Routine Follow-up within current clinic policy (initially day seven inclusive), **Then** one active claim freezes policy-derived or actual-decision provenance, deadline, policy revision, price and actual actor evidence. No fabricated Doctor DEFAULT or automatic booking is created.
2. **Given** completion on 1 November Cairo-local date, even if the original service date was earlier, **When** the clinic window is seven days, **Then** 8 November is included and 9 November is outside by default unless a separately authorized specific-date exception is audited. An Owner change to the clinic window affects **new claims** (also after release), not confirmed claims. Completion date, not a 168-hour duration or original service date, establishes day zero.
3. **Given** an absent, non-Completed, wrong-patient/Doctor/clinic, opted-out, expired or already-consumed source, or a source that is itself a Follow-up, **When** preferential care is requested, **Then** no unqualified Follow-up is created. Nonqualifying care is booked as New Examination under ordinary current pricing and all normal booking guards; denied foreign records reveal no clinical details.
4. **Given** a source already reserved by a Booked, Waiting or In Examination preferential claim, **When** another reservation competes, **Then** it conflicts without a second active claim or New Examination fallback, even for a different service date. A Completed consumption permanently prevents another preferential use from the source.
5. **Given** a confirmed claim, **When** source/policy/price changes, **Then** original entitlement, source evidence and price stay frozen. Any authorized staff member may separately authorize an extension only with a mandatory reason, specific later/non-shortening Cairo date and append-only actual-actor/source/claim/policy/revision audit before an outside-deadline move. Every Receptionist extension respects current genuine Doctor restrictions, including later restrictions. Extension neither moves nor reprices; release/rebooking inherits none of it.
6. **Given** synthetic claim lifecycle records, **When** cancellation, exceptional In Examination cancellation with retained Visit, No-show, completion or a competing new claim is exercised at the persistence boundary, **Then** release/consumption is inseparable from its qualifying terminal outcome, no failed outcome releases entitlement, and at most one active claim and one ever-Completed consumption survive. A later new claim after release uses current eligibility and price.
7. **Given** source decisions DEFAULT, OPTED_OUT and DEADLINE, **When** Doctor, Manager or ordinary authorized Receptionist records them, **Then** actual actor evidence is retained; non-DEFAULT needs a reason and DEADLINE a Cairo date ≥ completion. Receptionists may revise prior Receptionist-authored decisions with immutable before/after audit. All Receptionist paths deny replacement/modification/bypass of genuine Doctor OPTED_OUT/DEADLINE; only authenticated Doctor revision changes that restriction. Operators and clinical-content writes by Receptionists deny; operational DTOs expose no Doctor narrative.
8. **Given** a permitted nonqualifying Follow-up fallback, **When** staff have not explicitly acknowledged its actual New Examination type and ordinary price, **Then** no booking commits; staff must review and acknowledge that result before confirmation, which rechecks current pricing, eligibility and all ordinary guards. Staff must explicitly choose or reaffirm discount mode/value and any required reason against the ordinary base, including an explicit NONE choice; earlier inputs remain available for review but are not silently applied or discarded.
9. **Given** a qualifying Completed source, **When** authorized staff record a reasoned DEADLINE on/after its Cairo completion, **Then** the specific inclusive deadline may shorten or extend the current default without an invented maximum. Before-completion/missing-date/missing-reason inputs deny. A genuine Doctor DEADLINE replaces the source default and limits every Receptionist path; a Doctor OPTED_OUT blocks it. A source revision never rewrites existing frozen claims; confirmed extensions are separate and non-shortening.
10. **Given** a qualifying source that is already Completed at confirmation, **When** staff request preferential Follow-up for its Cairo completion date, **Then** day zero is eligible through the authorized deadline, subject to every ordinary booking guard. An intended date before completion never qualifies, including under Doctor exceptions/extensions. If the source Appointment's original service date equals the target date, its counted Completed status blocks a second appointment; if its original service date is older, it does not itself occupy the target day's allowance. Thus a source finishing after midnight may support day-zero Follow-up on its completion date only if no other counted patient/day appointment or other guard blocks it.
11. **Given** a qualifying source and a selected date beyond the clinic default, **When** authorized staff approve that exact NEW-booking date, **Then** Doctor requires a reason while Receptionist may omit it; both require immutable actual actor/role/tenant/source/date/policy/source-revision/time audit linked to confirmation. Receptionist cannot exceed a genuine Doctor DEADLINE or bypass Doctor OPTED_OUT, even with a reason. Stale/revoked/consumed/pre-completion or ordinary booking conflicts deny atomically; this exception is no confirmed-claim extension.

### User Story 5 - Retain trustworthy foundations for later care (Priority: P2)

As clinic staff, I need future attendance, admission and clinical work to use the original booked identity, date, entitlement and history, so that midnight, retries or later corrections cannot create duplicate or misleading care records.

**Why this priority**: These foundations unblock Wave 3, but they do not deliver queue or clinical operating commands in Wave 2.

**Independent Test**: Verify retained synthetic queue, Visit, approval and day-review records, permissions and competing changes at the persistence boundary. These are foundation proofs, not evidence that future admission, clinical or reconciliation commands exist.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** synthetic Waiting records across MAIN and EMERGENCY on different dates, **When** memberships and revisions are retained, **Then** each clinic/date/lane has one dated queue, each Waiting Appointment has exactly one active matching entry, and original arrival/rank/history are preserved without ghost or duplicate entries. Emergency ordering is independent of MAIN; only ordinary MAIN Routine-head admission may advance the Routine cursor.
2. **Given** shared Doctor capacity one and competing simulated admissions from different lanes/dates, **When** persistence guards are exercised, **Then** at most one additional active examination can be recorded. Pause/capacity belong to the single Doctor across all lanes/dates, not separately to each queue.
3. **Given** a Doctor approval for an additional examination, **When** another target/date/lane, expired/revoked/consumed approval or stale relevant revision is used, **Then** it is unusable. Only its exact scope can consume it once with the related admission record; Receptionist/operator explicit revocation is denied. No blanket approval or expiry duration is invented.
4. **Given** an Appointment, **When** ordinary booking or attendance data is saved, **Then** no Visit is created. A valid admission-associated foundation permits exactly one Visit; a retained exceptionally cancelled Visit survives, and Doctor post-terminal corrections retain immutable old/new values, actor/role and second-precision time without reopening the Appointment.
5. **Given** dated Booked, Waiting and In Examination records and a day-review record, **When** Cairo midnight passes or review metadata is amended, **Then** original dates/states/history stay intact. Review is not a seventh status; a final close cannot have unresolved Waiting and cannot end an in-progress Visit or imply No-show.
6. **Given** retained appointments, number assignments and activity records, **When** date/status/lane/family and later reporting query foundations are checked, **Then** independent patient identities and original service-day versus event-day attribution remain recoverable. Cancelled retained Visits are not Completed service value; reporting workflows remain later scope.
7. **Given** representative existing synthetic records, **When** the persistence foundations are added or an attempted change fails, **Then** identifiers, relationships, history and access protections survive; failure leaves no partial successful change or misleading completion evidence.

8. **Given** an admitted synthetic Visit, **When** persistence records completion evidence, **Then** only a positive explicit examination-occurred assertion bound to actual actor ID/role, tenant, Appointment/Visit and server time can accompany Completed and preferential consumption atomically. Missing/false assertion, wrong state, duplicate or competing cancellation fails without partial evidence. Receptionist operational attestation creates no Doctor note, signature, source decision or ClinicalRevision; B-16 owns the future command.

### Edge Cases

- **Rounding at the premium threshold**: compare the final rounded Urgent net and its base to the current standard base; equality fails, including equality introduced by rounding (US2.3–5).
- **Empty configuration**: no initial price, period, duration or cutoff is fabricated; eligible Open needs no timed interval, while timed or Urgent eligibility cannot be inferred from missing hours (US2.1/4, US3.1–3/9).
- **Partial interval intersections**: manual precedence suppresses any intersecting generated candidate, not only an identical start; capacity considers all simultaneous intersections, while adjacent endpoints remain nonoverlapping (US3.4–5).
- **Cairo midnight and daylight saving**: booking uses explicit local service date, Follow-up uses completion day zero, repeated/missing wall times require rejection or valid disambiguation, and old queues never migrate automatically (US3.6, US4.2, US5.5).
- **Concurrent changes**: archive/booking, two patient/day bookings, number allocation, price revision, schedule activation/new booking, Follow-up extension/claim and release/consumption use one consistent valid outcome or deny without partial success (US1.3–6, US2.5, US3.8, US4.4–6).
- **Uncertain result after submission**: an unknown commit outcome must be reconciled through the original authorized request/result; it must not be treated as proof of rollback (US1.4).
- **Archived preconfirmed records**: no new ordinary or preferential Appointment while archived; existing Appointment history remains and archive alone cannot invalidate later lawful same-ID operations. This grants no general Receptionist access to archived patient or Doctor-only records (US1.5–6, US4.7).
- **Future workflow dependencies**: blocked conflicting schedule activation remains blocked until authorized Wave 3 changes can resolve it; no temporary cancellation/rescheduling shortcut is added to Wave 2 (US3.7–8).
