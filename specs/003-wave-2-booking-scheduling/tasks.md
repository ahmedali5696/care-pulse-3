**Pinned decisions** (the approved documents leave these implicit; they are fixed here so implementers do not guess — each is listed again for reviewer sign-off at the end of this file)
- **P1 Policy-head lock order (all Wave 2 code)**: `SCHEDULE` → `CAPACITY` → `FOLLOWUP` → `PRICE`, always after the live-authorization barrier and the patient lifecycle lock, before day/counter, Appointment, source Visit and claim rows. `SCHEDULE` is the common publication barrier: bookings take it `FOR SHARE`, schedule activation takes it `FOR UPDATE`.

- **P3 Working day & optional periods**: Open clinics may configure working days without inventing operating hours (`spec.md` W2-FR-08/09, `contracts/http.md` §4). Without modifying D-04 seed v1 (`DEFAULTS["SCHEDULE"]` stays verbatim on `version = 1`), B-07's published `SCHEDULE` `payload` JSONB (`version >= 2`, already accepted by `0017_d04_policy_versions.py`'s `value ?& ARRAY[...]` check) carries additive sorted arrays `working_weekdays` (integers `1..7`), `open_override_dates` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"` dates opened without hours for Open mode) and `manual_slot_dates` (`"YYYY-MM-DD"` dates with an explicitly configured date-specific manual replacement set, **including empty `[]`**). On legacy versions lacking `working_weekdays`, `effective_working_weekdays` defaults to weekdays having `WORK` rows (so seed v1 has `[]`). For date `d`: `resolved_periods = OVERRIDE_WORK periods on d if any, else [] if (d in holidays or d in open_override_dates), else weekly WORK periods of d.isoweekday() if d.isoweekday() in effective_working_weekdays else []`. Weekly `BREAK`s of `d.isoweekday()` apply to every period on `d`, including `OVERRIDE_WORK` periods. `is_working_day(d)` is `True` if `d not in holidays` and (`d` has `OVERRIDE_WORK` periods or `d in open_override_dates` or `d.isoweekday() in effective_working_weekdays`), else `False`.
- **P4 Eligibility**: on a working day (`is_working_day(d)` True), **Open** needs no hours and **Exact-Time** needs non-empty `resolved_periods` yielding valid candidates. On a holiday (`d in holidays`) or non-working day, ordinary booking requires `holiday_booking_enabled`: if enabled, **Open** needs no hours, while **Exact-Time** additionally requires explicit date-specific `OVERRIDE_WORK` periods on `d`; if disabled, both Open and Exact-Time are denied.
- **P5 Affected bookings and occupancy** (approved Product Owner decision, 2026-10-28): proposed date, operating-period and break invalidation is **blocking** for future `Booked` and confirmed `Waiting` (`service_date >= Cairo today`), including dates with **no `Booked`**. `In Examination` and `Completed` preserve ongoing/completed care: proposed date/period/break invalidation does **not** retrospectively disqualify them or block activation on its own. Independently check **all future dates with counted timed appointments** in `Booked`, `Waiting`, `In Examination` or `Completed`, including dates with **no `Booked`** and regardless of whether overlap/capacity settings changed; a proposed effective overlap limit below actual peak occupancy **blocks** activation. Count all four statuses in the peak, preserving their existing intervals and states. `Cancelled`/`No-show` never reserve capacity; cutoff exposure applies to `Booked` only. Never automatically cancel, reschedule, reprice, change status or rewrite an existing interval.

- **P7 Stale detection for activation**: preview stores `service_day_revision = 0` (unused); the **recomputed complete affected set + captured per-appointment revisions + policy revisions** compared under the barrier is the stale detector. No new service-day table.
