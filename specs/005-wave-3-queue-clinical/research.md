## Decisions

### Reuse F-01, extending only the implemented operation

- **Decision:** Keep existing signed BFF/API transport, independent database proof, actual login/backend/transaction binding, live authorization and protected nonce lifecycle. Add exact method/path/purpose, signed resource extraction, permitted stage/recovery, RLS/column/function grants, receipt namespace and audit/outbox scope for each owning operation through reviewed forward-only evolution.
- **Rationale:** `apps/web/auth/proxy.ts:39–73`, API `main.py:91–174`, `identity/boundary.py:228–290`, `identity/authorization.py:136–178` and `identity/runtime_identity.py:33–80` already implement the boundary. `0043_f01_writer_boundary.py:373–386` fences unsupported queue/terminal/day-review writers. [F-01](../../docs/f01-runtime-identity.md) and master §8 require operation-specific current-head proof; a valid proof does not validate arbitrary business arguments.
- **Alternatives considered:** Another identity layer, broad tenant activation, direct browser/private-API access or restored shared-runtime/GUC authority would duplicate or weaken established contracts. Do not weaken 0046 resource/audit isolation.

### Reuse domain substrate and transaction-taking primitives

- **Decision:** Extend existing Queue/Visit/DayReview metadata and use patient lifecycle, booking date/slot/number/price, policy heads, B-10 qualification/claim commands, tenant receipts and audit/outbox helpers. Individual and bulk changes call the same domain primitives in one caller transaction.
- **Rationale:** `queue/tables.py:20–119`, `visits/tables.py:20–128`, migrations 0025/0027 and `notifications/evidence.py:13–87` supply substrate. They do not establish protected product routes. B-12 precedes B-11; admission alone creates Visit. B-16 owns completion and In Examination exceptional cancel.
- **Alternatives considered:** Parallel intake booking logic, an entitlement engine, generic repository or new queue/worker service increases scope without a need. No dependency or infrastructure added.

### Preserve B-10 while adding Doctor clinical PATCH

- **Decision:** One strict Visit PATCH dispatcher selects exactly one closed source-decision or Doctor clinical payload family; BFF, API and database scope must agree. Preserve existing legacy/v2 B-10 schemas, operation/receipt family and live field-safe replay. Deny mixed/duplicate/unknown fields before execution; never widen VISIT_FOLLOWUP authority to clinical content.
- **Rationale:** `main.py:112–124`, `visits/routes.py:94–114` and the BFF Visit catch-all currently bind PATCH to VISIT_FOLLOWUP only. Two independent handlers cannot select themselves by body. Operational source-decision revision is distinct from clinical revision.
- **Alternatives considered:** A broad permissive Visit DTO silently grants clinical scope; a second conflicting PATCH handler cannot dispatch safely. A separate unapproved clinical route is unnecessary when the established PATCH can strictly dispatch.

### B-18 approved current onsite evidence (PO 2026-11-23)

- **Approved outcome and decision:** B-18 can adjust a lawful existing Paid Urgent on the same Cairo day, including when the authenticated actor is Doctor or the Patient was archived after booking, preserving original ID/lane/premium. The Product Owner decided on **2026-11-23** that **every** such reschedule requires a fresh positive physical-presence attestation from the **currently authorized acting staff member** within that command. Current onsite, work-period/break/holiday/slot and PRICE/SCHEDULE revision conditions continue at commit. Urgent **creation** remains Receptionist-only and cross-day same-ID movement forbidden (PRD FR-012/BR-016a, Domain INV-04/37/38, master B-18).
- **Evidence distinction:** Original Urgent intake `Waiting` and QueueEntry `attested_by_staff_id`/`attested_at` establish historical attendance, not the current reschedule's fresh assertion. The request must affirm physical presence positively for that operation; server-side authorization identifies the actual actor and validates the assertion against that same Appointment inside the reschedule transaction. Persist immutable evidence of that actor ID/role, tenant, Appointment and server operation time with the successful reschedule, alongside existing history/audit/receipt. A prior intake assertion, separate earlier attestation, active entry alone, caller-supplied actor/time or evidence for another Appointment cannot satisfy this command.
- **Acceptance/disposition:** Positive current-operation assertion and all existing eligibility guards permit a lawful same-ID change; missing/false/historical-only/mismatched evidence or revoked acting authorization rejects atomically with no successful move/evidence/history/event/receipt. A lost acknowledgement may replay only the already committed, originally authorized command result; it must not execute another move with reused presence evidence. The **B-18 onsite product-decision blocker is resolved**, but T049/T052 and relevant race acceptance remain **UNVERIFIED** until actual executable proof. No geolocation checks, separate expiry/freshness TTL, new staff role, special Doctor restriction or other Urgent rule is added.

### Persist bulk preview through D-09 receipts

- **Wire/size decision:** The preview request carries the complete unique selection within the existing 1 MiB BFF/FastAPI mutation-body boundary. The preview response's `included` is B-19's concrete wire name for the master's generic `eligible[]` (with `excluded`, exact counts and revisions); the master's optional `affected[]` is not a B-19 alias. The later strict commit body contains only server-issued `preview_id` and typed `confirmation:"RESCHEDULE"`, alongside the established `Idempotency-Key` header. The server loads all original selected IDs, both partitions, original/destination dates and revision evidence from the immutable, actor/tenant/preview-operation-scoped receipt, and revalidates them against current state. Do not echo the potentially large receipt JSON through the browser or broaden the transport limit. A changed selection/destination requires a new preview; unknown commit body fields reject.
- **Decision:** Store an immutable same-actor/tenant bulk preview in the existing receipt JSONB result, with server receipt UUID as preview_id, original day, **complete original authorized selection**, distinct included eligible and excluded ineligible identities/reasons, destination and all compared revisions. A nonempty, duplicate-free selected-ID array is strict input validation (422 for empty/duplicate; never deduplicate); a valid different-day all-excluded cohort may receive an explanatory `eligible_count=0` preview but cannot produce a successful zero-move commit. Add a narrow preview-ID read capability only for the matching bulk-commit scope, plus preview receipt-ID recovery. Commit revalidates original selection, partition identities, bound evidence and live guards, then moves only the unchanged eligible included subset atomically; an unchanged original exclusion is not itself a commit conflict. No new preview table, product eligibility rule or invented TTL.
- **Rationale:** `0004_evidence.py:222–275` supplies immutable receipt rows; `identity/receipts.py:41–46,86–128` returns generated receipt ID on insert. Existing lookup `identity/receipts.py:154–185` does **not** select that receipt ID, and 0042 RLS permits only current operation/key. Preview recovery must retrieve the **same original receipt UUID** through a narrowly scoped lookup instead of issuing a new one; cross-operation preview access for commit also requires the matching constrained reader. A single result_revision cannot establish all record freshness. The UI must invalidate typed confirmation after any selected-ID, destination or reason edit; only the committed server-side snapshot is an executable command target.
- **Alternatives considered:** Browser-supplied eligibility, a second preview database/table, or treating the preview as an authorization grant adds risk or unnecessary machinery. Generic receipt access must remain closed.

### Carry original local intervals and classify bulk capacity conflicts jointly

- **Decision:** For each Exact-Time B-19 row, carry its original Africa/Cairo local start and end clock times onto the different destination service date; Open rows remain day-only. Derive and bind the resulting destination UTC instants server-side, without interval overrides, shifting or auto-assignment. Reuse existing destination-date, manual/generated slot, break, holiday and capacity guards first. Resolve local endpoints through the existing Exact-Time resolver: a nonexistent or ambiguous destination clock that cannot resolve uniquely is an operational exclusion, without copying a source UTC offset, choosing a fold or applying B-20's cutoff adjustment policy.
- **Decision:** A candidate is an otherwise-eligible selected Exact-Time row passing every individual guard against existing confirmed destination bookings. Evaluate all candidates together using half-open `[start,end)` intervals and the configured same-time booking capacity `C` (`1` when overlap is off). For each elementary time segment, compute existing booking load plus all active candidates. In one order-independent pass, exclude every candidate active in any segment with load greater than `C`, using the operational reason `destination capacity conflict`; never exclude an independent candidate or an Open row for that reason, or displace existing bookings. Persist derived intervals and exact partitions in the existing immutable preview receipt. Commit fresh-validates the bound evaluation and moves all included rows atomically, with no re-exclusion, ranking or partial move; changed partitions, bound evidence or included-set infeasibility reject the entire commit.
- **Rationale:** The two explicit specification clarifications settle interval derivation and allocation policy. `apps/api/carepulse/policies/slots.py:30–55,151–200` already resolves Cairo endpoints and validates offered slots; `apps/api/carepulse/policies/intervals.py:8–35` supplies half-open UTC semantics. The single-row validator in `apps/api/carepulse/policies/slots_service.py:100–138` uses a boolean availability helper at `:42–47`; neither identifies every participant in a batch conflict. A small pure segment/conflict classifier within the existing scheduling module is the necessary proposed extension; no dependency, service, new table or column is selected.
- **Alternatives considered:** Independent per-row checks miss candidate-versus-candidate conflicts. Sequential first-fit, booking-number/start-time/selection-order priority and iterative salvage introduce prohibited tie-breaking. Rejecting unrelated rows loses the required feasible subset. Fixed UTC offsets, automatically chosen folds, gap shifting and the B-20 cutoff resolver violate existing Exact-Time validation or preserved local clocks.

### Reuse protected destination occupancy and serialization

- **Decision:** Reuse the established counted-interval projection and policy/day serialization, extending only the exact authorized B-18/B-19 destination scopes through reviewed forward-only F-01 evolution. Preview reads current occupancy consistently without allocating numbers or moving records; commit locks the applicable day counters in date order, retains the shared number-allocation guard, fresh-reads destination occupancy after waits, and validates the entire included set before applying moves. Tentative number allocations remain transactional and roll back on rejection. Do not broaden Appointment visibility or reuse a historical runtime identity mechanism.
- **Rationale:** `apps/api/carepulse/policies/slots_service.py:27–39,109,131–137` consumes `f01_counted_appointment_intervals` under caller-held policy/day-counter authority. `db/migrations/versions/0046_f01_audit_integrity.py:363–391` currently limits that function to SLOTS/GET and APPOINTMENT_BOOKING/POST and counts Exact-Time Booked/Waiting/In Examination/Completed; unchanged scope cannot serve new rescheduling commands. Slot capacity is distinct from admission capacity. The existing current-boundary projection must be extended narrowly rather than assumed available, and real PostgreSQL races must prove its interaction with booking/policy writers.
- **Alternatives considered:** Broad table reads leak resources and bypass operation scope; per-row transactions lose batch atomicity; a preview allocation creates forbidden number effects. A preview remains advisory, with immutable evidence and mandatory live commit revalidation rather than a reservation or new TTL.

### Preserve ordered transactions, receipts and honest recovery

- **Decision:** Retain master §2 lock classes and the detailed applicable [Wave 2 transaction order](../003-wave-2-booking-scheduling/contracts/transactions.md), including policy-head ordering and existing-Appointment-before-source rules. Deterministically lock multi-record/date work, fresh-read after waiting, compare revisions inside commit. Domain result, history/audit/outbox/receipt commit together. Every retry has fresh signed/live authorization and nonce; unknown outcome is reconciled under the original key.
- **Rationale:** Capacity, lane head, number, slot and source/claim writers must compose with actual triggers. Real PostgreSQL lock observations must prove critical interleavings. Nonce consumption may be separately durable even when the business transaction rolls back.
- **Alternatives considered:** Separate replacement/bulk commits, unguarded preview-write, arbitrary sleep/retry, new-key replay after lost response or stale receipt disclosure violates integrity or confidentiality.

### Configuration and later delivery remain bounded

- **Decision:** Confirmation TTL is configured, persisted on issue and checked at consumption; absent/invalid policy denies issuing/using confirmations. Only labeled synthetic fixture values are chosen during authorized testing. Commit minimal operational events now; B-23/B-24 deliver/filter realtime/push and B-25 owns scheduling/retries.
- **Rationale:** Spec clarification and master §2 already defer production numerical approval. Ordinary one-capacity admission needs no extra-case confirmation. BR-019's approved Doctor-origin cancellation/rescheduling rule applies through B-16 In Examination cancellation, B-17 Booked/Waiting cancellation, B-18 individual reschedule and B-19 bulk reschedule (one minimal event per successfully moved Appointment, atomic/no duplicates). Doctor manual No-show emits no Receptionist-review event; its normal audit/status still applies. Doctor-authored source reasons and clinical narrative are excluded from operational delivery.
- **Alternatives considered:** Fixed TTL, new settings control, production defaults, automatic midnight carry, network calls during a transaction or implementing a scheduler now would invent policy or transfer ownership.

### Bind receipt identity to the complete command target

- **Decision:** New command digests include normalized operation/resource identifiers from path/query, including target/day/preview identity, plus canonical typed body. Verify the saved result's target relationship before replay success. Preserve existing B-10 digest compatibility.
- **Rationale:** A body-only hash permits same actor/key/body reuse across visible URL targets. B-10 already hashes Visit ID plus normalized body (`visits/eligibility.py:504–508`). Transport raw-body signature and canonical command digest have different jobs.
- **Alternatives considered:** Body-only digests or checking merely that the original result is visible cannot distinguish two authorized resources.

### Preserve DayReview management authority

- **Decision:** Authorized staff may create initial review/close. Any existing-review revision update, including amendment/reopen or later close, remains Owner-only. Original same-key recovery reads the committed result rather than making another amendment.
- **Rationale:** PRD BR-049 separates management amendment from initial review; BR-036 does not delegate it. `0027_d11_day_review.py:440–449` already restricts existing-review updates to DOCTOR_OWNER.
- **Alternatives considered:** Treating amendment as a universal staff action broadens established permissions without authority.

### Apply the 2026-11-22 Product Owner checklist approvals

- **CHK007 / B-18 and B-19:** B-18 forbids already-Waiting Routine same-service-day rescheduling in Open or Exact-Time, including interval-only changes; B-13 `ManualRoutinePlacement` owns within-day Routine ordering. Valid cross-day Waiting→Booked requires new attendance, while independently eligible Booked and permitted same-day Urgent individual reschedules remain lawful. Separately, B-19 requires a destination Africa/Cairo service date DIFFERENT from the selected cohort's original date: an original-day destination rejects the ENTIRE bulk preview request, including Booked-only or mixed cohorts, before per-row eligibility, with no successful preview, counts, receipt or business effects. Only a valid different-day destination produces exact included/excluded selected identities, matching counts and per-row exclusion reasons; no separate bulk grant is introduced.
- **CHK009 / B-20:** Derive overdue exactly as `Appointment.service_date < current IANA Africa/Cairo date`; a same-day elapsed Exact-Time slot does not qualify. Manual Booked No-show needs current actual-actor authorization, verified absence, reason and atomic check-in exclusion; the separately configured automatic selected-day cutoff is not gated by this manual predicate.
- **CHK037 / B-20 with B-25:** Resolve the selected service-day B-07 cutoff HH:MM under IANA Africa/Cairo: repeated local instant uses the **later** occurrence (`fold=1`), a nonexistent local time advances to the **first valid instant after the gap**, and the result is compared against authoritative UTC time. Guard each Booked→No-show with atomic claim/evidence/receipt updates and replay/concurrent-run deduplication; retain retryability for still-eligible rows after interrupted or partially committed runs. B-20 owns selection/transition; B-25 invokes/schedules/retries. No new scheduler, daily one-shot lockout, settings field or fixed UTC offset.

They close the former product-decision clarification gap only; they do not modify approved PRD/Domain/System authorities, prove executable behavior or replace independent review.
