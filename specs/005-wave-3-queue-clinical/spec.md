### User Story 1 - Check in and view dated queues (Priority: P1)

As staff, I confirm a booked patient's presence and see their actual Waiting position separately from their booking number. **Owner: B-12.**

**Why this priority**: Attendance and dated queues precede admission and immediate-intake reuse.

**Independent Test**: Check in existing bookings under booked-order/FIFO policies, including an archived patient's booking, and inspect dates, ordering and absence of Visits.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** valid pre-existing Booked, including a booking made today, an overdue prior-day Booked still awaiting reconciliation, or one whose patient was later archived, **When** Doctor or Receptionist confirms physical presence, **Then** it becomes Waiting with exactly one dated entry, server arrival and committed Doctor-alert event, preserving number/price and creating no Visit. Concurrent/repeated check-in produces no duplicate entry/alert.
2. **Given** Waiting Routine/Urgent/Emergency and unarrived bookings, **When** a day is selected, **Then** only Waiting occupies positions: MAIN Urgent FIFO ahead of Routine under that day's policy, and independent Emergency FIFO. Booking number is not position.
3. **Given** booked-order Routine, **When** a lower-numbered patient arrives, **Then** it is late only when its preliminary number is at or behind the selected original-day Routine cursor advanced by an actual ordinary Routine-head admission; otherwise booked-number order applies. A late arrival goes after the first `min(late_depth, Routine Waiting count)` Routine entries. With zero Routine Waiting there is no entry to pass; with fewer than late-depth entries it goes behind all existing Routine Waiting; with late-depth zero it goes ahead of Routine Waiting (never ahead of Urgent). Midnight never moves old Waiting to today's queue or changes its status/date.

### User Story 9 - Move an eligible same-day cohort together (Priority: P2)

As Doctor/Manager/ordinary Receptionist, I preview an authorized selection sharing one original service day, inspect its eligible included and ineligible excluded subsets, and atomically move the included subset. **Owner: B-19.**

**Why this priority**: Bulk convenience retains individual safeguards.

**Independent Test**: Preview mixed eligibility, commit the unchanged included subset while original exclusions remain excluded, then introduce a stale included record or bound condition and verify zero movement.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a selected cohort sharing one original `Africa/Cairo` service date, **When** B-19 bulk preview targets that same original date, **Then** reject the ENTIRE preview request before per-row eligibility, including Booked-only and mixed Booked/Waiting cohorts, with no successful preview, counts, receipt, number allocation, claim mutation, review event or other business effect. **When** the destination is a valid DIFFERENT Cairo-local service date, **Then** return the exact selected eligible/included and ineligible/excluded identities, `eligible_count` and `excluded_count` matching the respective row counts and summing to the authorized selection, and operational-only per-row exclusion reasons (including In Examination, terminal, Urgent Waiting and other ineligibles). Each Exact-Time row's destination interval is its own existing local start and end clock times carried onto the destination Cairo date, with no per-row override, shifting or server auto-assignment; Open rows remain day-only. A row whose carried-over interval fails a destination-date guard on its own against the existing destination state (holiday/work-period, break, overlap/capacity with existing confirmed bookings) is excluded with an operational reason. Remaining candidate rows are then evaluated jointly: a **candidate** is a selected, otherwise-eligible Exact-Time row that passed every per-row guard alone. Using half-open `[start,end)` intervals (touching endpoints do not overlap) and the configured same-time booking capacity `C` (one when overlap is off), compute for each elementary time segment the load = existing confirmed destination bookings + candidates active in it. A segment is **oversubscribed** when load exceeds `C`. Every candidate active in any oversubscribed segment participates in a **capacity conflict** and is excluded with the operational reason "destination capacity conflict", whether or not it was the overflowing one. Candidates active in no oversubscribed segment, and Open rows, are never excluded for this reason. Existing destination bookings are never moved, displaced or excluded. No priority or tie-breaking rule (booking number, start time, selection order, patient or any other attribute) is applied; the outcome is a single order-independent pass, identical for any selection order. Because every candidate active in an oversubscribed segment is removed, the included subset is jointly feasible. Reasons expose no other patient's or foreign booking identity or clinical data. B-18 lawful individual same-day Booked rescheduling remains permitted; CHK007 independently forbids already-Waiting Routine same-day rescheduling, including interval-only Open/Exact-Time changes. No Manager designation or separate bulk grant is needed.
2. **Given** an accepted different-day preview with an **original selection** (the complete authorized submitted cohort), an **included subset** (selected IDs eligible at preview) and an **excluded subset** (originally selected IDs ineligible at preview, retained with reasons), **When** the same authorized actor submits only that `preview_id` and typed `RESCHEDULE`, and the server recovers and revalidates the original selection, inclusion/exclusion identities, required revisions and relevant live guards from its immutable receipt, **Then** atomically move **every included eligible record and no excluded record**, preserving frozen prices/claims, new-day numbers and queue/history. An originally excluded record still excluded under unchanged bound evidence does not invalidate commit merely for being ineligible. For Doctor-origin commits, exactly one minimal Receptionist-review event per **moved** Appointment commits without receipt-recovery duplication; bulk cancellation remains absent.
3. **Given** an included record newly admitted or otherwise ineligible, a changed inclusion/exclusion identity, required bound revision or scheduling/claim/Doctor-precedence condition now stale, an included Exact-Time set no longer jointly feasible against live destination bookings and capacity (commit revalidates the same joint evaluation and never re-excludes, re-ranks or partially moves rows), a changed original selection/destination, or revoked/foreign actor, **When** commit revalidates the complete original selection, preview partitions, live authorization and applicable guards, **Then** reject the entire commit with **zero successful business/history/number/claim/audit/outbox/commit-receipt/review-event effects**; the already accepted preview evidence remains, but no partial included move occurs. An excluded record's continued exclusion alone is not a stale condition. A changed selection/destination requires a fresh valid preview and confirmation.
4. **Given** an empty ID selection or repeated Appointment ID, **When** preview input is validated, **Then** reject the invalid selection under the existing strict-schema/422 convention, without implicit deduplication or preview receipt. **Given** a nonempty unique, authorized different-day selection whose records are all ineligible, **When** previewed, **Then** return an explanatory valid preview with `eligible_count=0`, `included=[]`, all identities and reasons in `excluded`; attempting to commit that zero-included preview is rejected without a successful no-op commit, number, claim, audit or review event. These are input/commit validation outcomes, not new eligibility permissions.

**B-19 commit wire clarification:** The commit request sends only server-issued `preview_id` and exact typed `confirmation:"RESCHEDULE"` in JSON with the normal `Idempotency-Key` header. References above to matching selection, partition identities, dates and revisions mean **server-side recovery from the immutable preview receipt and fresh revalidation**, not resubmission of those values by the browser. Unknown or attempted replacement fields are rejected; selecting different IDs or a destination requires a new preview. One batch command receipt co-commits with individual moved-row histories and Doctor review events. The preview response names its eligible partition `included` (the concrete B-19 form of the master's generic `eligible[]`); `affected[]` is not a second B-19 array.

**Trace**: FR-041a/071/091; BR-019a/046; INV-14; AC-09/34/43; CON-12/16.

### User Story 10 - Reconcile overdue work without forced close (Priority: P2)

As staff, I review a chosen day, confirm absence and optionally close its review without terminating care. **Owner: B-20.**

**Why this priority**: Midnight must not manufacture absence or completion.

**Independent Test**: Cross midnight with Booked/Waiting/In Examination; compare previous-day and same-day elapsed Exact-Time slots, test manual absence/close and selected-day cutoff at Cairo DST folds/gaps, with concurrent invocation and interrupted/recovered processing.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** defaults, **When** midnight passes, **Then** no close/cutoff/status change/reassignment occurs; `requires_review` flags Booked/Waiting whose `service_date` is earlier than today's `Africa/Cairo` calendar date without a seventh status, and ongoing Visits retain their original date. An elapsed same-day Exact-Time slot alone never makes a record overdue.
2. **Given** an explicitly enabled Cairo selected-day cutoff, **When** its authoritative resolved instant is reached, **Then** only still-Booked unarrived records of that selected service date become No-show with atomic claim release/audit. Concurrent check-in yields Waiting or No-show, never both. Separately, Doctor or Receptionist may manually mark only an unarrived **overdue** Booked (`service_date < current Africa/Cairo date`) No-show with verified absence, required reason, actual actor/time, current authorization and transaction-safe check-in conflict validation, whether or not cutoff is configured. Same-day elapsed Exact-Time slots, Waiting and In Examination fail manual No-show. Without automatic cutoff, overdue Booked remains Booked until separately reconciled. Doctor-origin manual No-show retains ordinary status/audit evidence but emits no Receptionist-review event.
3. **Given** chosen-day Booked/Waiting/In Examination, **When** close finalizes, **Then** zero Waiting remains after lawful admission/reason-cancel/reschedule. Review alone leaves Booked unchanged; In Examination may finish later on original date. Authorized staff may create an initial day review/close, while audited amendment, reopening, or update of an existing review is Owner-only (not delegated to Manager or ordinary Receptionist) and never reopens terminal appointments.
4. **Given** prior-day Waiting still present, **When** admitted, **Then** fresh presence/shared-capacity safeguards apply; no insertion into today's ranks or false No-show to close yesterday.
5. **Given** a selected Cairo day with a configured cutoff at a repeated local time, **When** resolving the cutoff, **Then** the later occurrence (`fold=1`) governs; for a nonexistent time the first valid local instant after the transition governs. Do not apply before the resulting authoritative instant or against another date. Repeated/concurrent same-day invocation or matching recovery produces no duplicate Appointment transitions, claim releases, audits, events or successful receipts; interrupted/partial processing remains safely retryable for still-eligible records without a one-shot daily lockout.

**Trace**: FR-035/092/106–108; BR-027/048/049; INV-11/17/30; AC-19/33/41/42; CON-08/13/32.

### Edge Cases

- Dates/lanes compete for shared capacity; filtered views never hide active examinations from capacity accounting.
- New Urgent changes MAIN head; Emergency cannot displace MAIN. Reorder/cancel/revisions invalidate stale targets/confirmations.
- Late Routine with zero/fewer-than-depth entries or late-depth zero follows Story 1's bounded insertion rule; an unpassed lower number retains booked order. A first next-operating-day QueueDay snapshots the latest policy effective for its date under the serialized policy/day guard, including when publication races creation; existing QueueDay snapshots never change.
- Pause/mode/Manager revocation/confirmation expiry/tenant suspension race protected work; stale screens cannot authorize it. Suspension or disabling an actor mid-examination denies newly attempted completion/exceptional cancellation by that actor until live access is restored; the ongoing Visit remains unchanged. Any subsequent permitted action requires a currently authorized actual actor and applicable attestation.
- Midnight preserves service day for value; Cairo completion date separately supplies Follow-up day zero.
- Archive blocks new intake/replacement IDs but preserves lawful existing-ID check-in/admission/reschedule.
- Touching exact intervals do not overlap; manual precedence, breaks, date-specific hours and overlap capacity apply. Holiday permission never permits holiday Urgent; Open invents no hours/day cap.
- Walk-in OFF cannot be bypassed by fused booking/check-in; real same-day bookings need no arbitrary lead time.
- Later Doctor restriction does not retroactively cancel frozen claims but constrains later Receptionist actions; release/rebook inherits no extension.
- Completion/cancel/release/new-claim races preserve one outcome/Visit and at most one completed preferential use.
- Lost acknowledgements require authorized reconciliation, not blind duplicate mutations.
- Routine Waiting same-day reschedule denies in both scheduling modes; B-13 placement is the within-day ordering operation. Exact-Time slot expiry on the current Cairo date does not make an appointment overdue for manual No-show.
- Bulk destination capacity conflicts: with `C = 1`, any two candidates whose half-open intervals intersect are both excluded; with `C > 1`, all candidates active in a segment where existing bookings plus candidates exceed `C` are excluded (e.g., `C = 2` with two intersecting candidates and no existing booking includes both, but three mutually intersecting candidates, or two plus one existing booking, exclude every candidate active in the oversubscribed segment). Touching endpoints never conflict, and candidates elsewhere in the day are unaffected.
- Selected-day cutoff resolves Africa/Cairo DST fold/gap to one authoritative instant; retries and competing workers may finish remaining eligible records without duplicating committed effects.
- General Receptionist nested history/errors/audit/events never expose Doctor narrative; future full export is not general clinical access.
