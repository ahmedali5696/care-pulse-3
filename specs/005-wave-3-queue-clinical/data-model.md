# Wave 3 Data Model

Phase 1 proposed design, 2026-11-22; not DDL or runtime acceptance. Reuse [Wave 2 model](../003-wave-2-booking-scheduling/data-model.md), subject to the current [F-01 boundary](../../docs/f01-runtime-identity.md), approved amendments and [research prerequisites](research.md#upstream-prerequisite-register). Do not copy historical shared-runtime/GUC authorization assumptions into new work. [Transactions](contracts/transactions.md) defines atomic changes; [HTTP](contracts/http.md) defines projections.

## Shared representation

Existing UUID identities and non-null organization_id remain stable. Tenant-qualified foreign keys must validate Patient/Appointment/Visit/Doctor relationships together. FORCE RLS and effective column/function permissions apply under actual service logins; migration authority alone owns DDL. No cascading clinical/history deletion.

UTC timestamptz stores arrival/admission/completion/audit/expiry; Cairo DATE stores service day and deadline. `overdue` is derived strictly from `Appointment.service_date < current IANA Africa/Cairo date`, never elapsed same-day Exact-Time slot alone; `requires_review` uses that boundary for prior-day Booked/Waiting, while In Examination remains separately visible without an inferred absence verdict. An enabled selected-day No-show cutoff resolves its configured wall-clock time to one authoritative UTC instant using IANA Africa/Cairo (`fold=1` for a repeated time, first valid local instant after a nonexistent time); no fixed UTC offset or new cutoff settings field is used. Appointment labels remain exactly Booked, Waiting, In Examination, Completed, Cancelled, No-show. Review flags, queue disposition, confirmation state and claim lifecycle introduce no Appointment state. EGP is Decimal/NUMERIC with final-net ROUND_HALF_UP and decimal-string wire values; snapshots never reprice on move.

Expected revisions are separate typed tokens, not client authority: patient lifecycle, Appointment/history, QueueDay/entry, Doctor control, each policy head, Visit clinical, source decision, claim and claim-authorization revision. Only applicable tokens are required by each command; the complete compared set is retained for bulk previews and case confirmations. A clinical correction does not stand in for an operational source decision.

**B-14 confirmation validity/handoff (existing records, no new status):** A usable approval is an `ISSUED`, unexpired, not revoked or consumed Doctor record for the **exact** tenant/Doctor/Patient/Appointment/original Cairo day/lane and matching live Appointment, QueueDay, Doctor-control, CAPACITY and ADMISSION revisions plus issuer-security eligibility. Serialize issuance by Doctor control, selected QueueDay and target Appointment; check for an already **currently usable** matching case before inserting. Same actor/operation/key replay returns the same historical UUID, with no renewal; distinct-key reissuance while a valid approval exists conflicts. An old expired or bound-revision-stale `ISSUED` row remains historical/ineligible and does not block a new-key approval after fresh checks. A consumed-only partial unique index does **not** guarantee this invariant. Under a separately authenticated admission-authorized session, a Receptionist may read **only** the matching usable confirmation UUID for the selected visible Waiting case through a narrowly authorized operational projection; no Doctor session or issuance/revoke privilege is conferred. B-14 owns the projection; B-15 independently validates and consumes the one-use ID.

**B-18 timed same-ID occupancy:** The target Appointment's stored old interval and proposed target interval are two versions of **one** booking, not simultaneous occupancy. Under the destination Doctor/date schedule barrier and locked Appointment, compare the proposed interval once against all **other** counted destination intervals at the configured overlap/capacity; if an existing F-01 projection returns the target old interval, exclude **only that exact authorized target ID** from the old occupancy set before adding the proposed interval. Do not remove another booking or grant a broad exclusion/query. This is an operation-scoped transactional comparison, not a new slot record or additional Appointment column; races with a third-party booking or capacity publication must recheck and reject true conflicts.

### Bulk preview: reuse receipts

B-19 uses **two distinct staff-bound D-09 receipts** for successful preview and successful batch commit. The preview receipt's single generated UUID is `preview_id` and its immutable result stores the entire original selected cohort, `included`/`excluded` partitions, reason/count/revision/day evidence. The later commit's strict JSON contains only `preview_id` and `confirmation:"RESCHEDULE"` (plus the existing `Idempotency-Key` header), and the server reads and checks that matching preview receipt inside its authorized commit boundary. There is **one committed batch command receipt**, not one receipt per moved Appointment; every included move separately appends its own immutable AppointmentHistory row and, for a Doctor action, its own minimal review event in the same transaction. The generic master `eligible[]` preview concept is spelled `included` on the B-19 wire; no duplicate alias or `affected[]` response is required for this subset preview.

B-19 validates the destination **before** per-row eligibility and preview creation: if it equals the cohort's original Africa/Cairo service date, deny the **entire preview request** even for a Booked-only selection, with no successful preview, receipt, number allocation, claim mutation or review event. This is distinct from CHK007's B-18 individual already-Waiting Routine same-day prohibition; lawful B-18 individual Booked same-day reschedules remain permitted. The **original selection** is the complete authorized, nonempty, unique-ID cohort submitted for preview; an empty selection or duplicate Appointment ID is invalid strict input (422), never silently deduplicated. Only a different valid destination day creates an authorized D-09 immutable preview receipt with server UUID `preview_id`, actual tenant/actor/security revision, original cohort day, **exact original selected IDs**, distinct `included` eligible and `excluded` ineligible IDs/operational reasons, `eligible_count` and `excluded_count`, destination and required per-record/policy/queue/source/claim/authorization revisions. Counts equal the respective returned row lengths and sum to the authorized exact selection size; excluded identities/reasons must not expose unauthorized clinical or foreign resource data. All-excluded valid cohorts retain their reasons and `eligible_count=0` in a successful explanatory preview, but their commit cannot succeed as a no-op. Commit independently rechecks the original selection, both partition identities, required bound revisions, live authorization, eligibility and applicable scheduling/claim/Doctor-precedence guards; it atomically moves **only the unchanged eligible included subset**, not the entire original selection. An unchanged originally excluded item remains excluded and does not alone invalidate the commit; an included item newly ineligible or relevant bound condition stale rejects the whole commit with no successful effects. Do not add a bulk_reschedule grant or reuse schedule-policy preview rows with different ownership semantics.

Each Exact-Time row derives its destination interval by converting stored source endpoints to Africa/Cairo local start/end clock times and resolving those same clocks on the destination service date. Persist resolvable destination UTC `starts_at`/`ends_at` in the existing preview result and retain the source Appointment revision and local clocks; an unresolvable excluded row retains its clocks and operational reason without fabricated destination instants. Open rows remain day-only with no interval. No per-row override, shifting, automatic assignment or fixed-source-offset carry is allowed. Existing Exact-Time destination validation rejects a nonpositive, nonexistent or unresolved ambiguous interval; B-20's cutoff fold/gap policy does not apply.

After individual destination-date/slot/break/holiday/existing-booking guards, candidates are the remaining otherwise-eligible Exact-Time rows. Jointly evaluate half-open `[start,end)` intervals for existing confirmed destination bookings plus all candidates at configured capacity `C` (`1` when overlap is off). Exclude every candidate active in any elementary segment whose load exceeds `C`, once, with reason `destination capacity conflict`. Touching endpoints do not overlap. No priority, tie-breaking, iterative salvage or exclusion of independent candidates/Open rows is permitted; existing bookings remain unchanged. Bind exact derived intervals, partitions and relevant schedule/capacity evidence in the receipt. Commit revalidates the same bound evaluation against live occupancy and requires the entire included set jointly feasible; changed intervals/partitions/bound evidence or an infeasible included set reject all moves rather than creating a smaller batch.

Existing receipt lookup/RLS is scoped to the current operation/key and cannot already load another operation by UUID. Add only a preview-specific reader under authenticated bulk-commit scope, bound to signed preview_id, same tenant/actor and exact preview operation. If privileged SQL is necessary, fixed safe search_path and narrow EXECUTE/projection are mandatory. Generic receipt reads remain closed. Preview replay must recover the original UUID/result, not create another preview. Full comparison plus fresh live eligibility invalidates stale previews; no new time limit is selected. The existing F-01 counted-interval projection likewise requires a narrowly authorized destination-date extension for B-18/B-19; current SLOTS/booking authority cannot be assumed to cover these operations.

The present `identity/receipts.py` idempotency lookup reads only result/revision, **not** `idempotency_receipts.id`; B-19 preview recovery therefore must expose the existing UUID to the same authorized actor/operation/key without making another receipt. UI confirmation is invalidated when selection, destination or reason changes, because the server can evaluate only the immutable original selection attached to `preview_id` and has no access to unsent UI state.

## State and evidence transitions

| Command | Appointment / related transition | Required invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check-in | Booked→Waiting; create one active entry | Positive physical presence, original date/number/price retained, no Visit |
| Immediate intake | New ID→Waiting plus snapshot/number/entry | Routine toggle/date/slot; Emergency independent lane; Urgent Receptionist-only onsite same-day premium/work-period guards |
| Admission | Waiting→In Examination; close entry, create Visit; consume confirmation if active count already ≥1 | Shared capacity across dates/lanes, current mode/unpaused, ordinary head or reasoned exception, fresh old-day presence; only ordinary MAIN Routine head advances cursor |
| Complete | In Examination→Completed; retain Visit, append positive attestation, consume active claim once | Actual staff identity, no invented Doctor content/decision; qualifying New Examination becomes source under upstream rules |
| Exceptional cancel | In Examination→Cancelled; retain Visit/content, release active claim | Doctor only, reason, no completed value/source, one Doctor-origin review event |
| Ordinary cancel / manual No-show | Booked/Waiting→Cancelled, or only unarrived prior-day Booked→No-show; close queue if applicable, release claim | Manual No-show requires strict earlier-than-current Cairo service date, verified absence, reason, real authorized actor, and check-in race guard regardless of cutoff setting; same-day Exact-Time expiry alone fails. Doctor manual No-show emits no Receptionist-review event |
| Selected-day cutoff | Current enabled policy+resolved IANA Cairo instant reached, still-unarrived Booked on that selected service date→No-show; release any active claim | Independent of manual overdue; atomically update per Appointment with audit/operational event/receipt only once. DST fold=1 or first valid gap instant; concurrent/repeated execution has no duplicate effects, and partial failures allow rechecking remaining Booked without day-level one-shot exclusion. | Replace Routine with Urgent | Old Booked/Waiting→Cancelled plus linked new Urgent ID→Waiting | One transaction, reason/typed confirmation, current Urgent/new-ID eligibility, new number/price; failure preserves old effects |
| Reschedule / bulk | Same-ID eligible Routine move; cross-day Waiting→Booked; detach old queue/fresh number/attendance; same-day Booked allowed subject to normal guards | An already-Waiting Routine cannot move on its same service day in Open or Exact-Time; use B-13 placement for ranking. Every same-day existing-ID Paid Urgent adjustment requires a new positive physical-presence assertion from its currently authorized acting staff member, bound to actual actor/Appointment/server operation time and checked inside the move transaction; original attendance alone is insufficient. Frozen price/entitlement, distinct prior extension when required, current Doctor precedence, all other same-day Urgent guards and no cross-day same-ID Urgent remain; bulk moves **all originally included eligible IDs or none**, never excluded IDs. Original selection/exclusion identities and bound revisions revalidated; stable exclusions are allowed, stale inclusion or guards abort. Doctor-origin bulk adds one atomic minimal Receptionist-review event per moved Appointment and none on rollback/replay |
| Doctor correction | Retained terminal outcome, next clinical revision | Immutable old/new actual-Doctor evidence, no claim/outcome reopening |
| Review/close | Review record only | Zero unresolved Waiting for close; Booked unchanged, In Examination continues on original day |

**B-18 onsite-evidence decision (2026-11-23):** The Product Owner requires a fresh positive physical-presence attestation for **every** same-day existing-ID Paid Urgent reschedule, by the presently authorized actual acting staff member. The server binds that assertion to the authenticated actor ID/role, tenant, specific Appointment and operation time, checks it with live authority and the current onsite/Cairo-day/work/slot/policy guards **inside the reschedule transaction**, and records immutable successful evidence with its history/audit. `QueueEntry.attested_by_staff_id`/`attested_at` and `Waiting` prove earlier attendance, not this new assertion. Missing/false, borrowed, mismatched or revoked-actor evidence denies with no successful effects; a receipt replay may only retrieve a previously committed result, never perform a second change on old presence evidence. The PO **decision blocker is resolved**, while implementation/evidence verification is pending. No new required storage column, TTL, geolocation check or narrower existing-ID actor role is selected; archived Patient and frozen Urgent premium/lane remain valid under prior guards.

Confirmation ISSUED may become CONSUMED once in successful admission or REVOKED by Doctor while unused. Expiry and any change to the bound Appointment, selected-lane QueueDay (including another patient's check-in or reorder), Doctor-control, capacity-policy or admission-policy revisions, or role/scope make it ineligible without granting another actor revocation power. Persist expiry from configured TTL and evaluate against the authoritative PostgreSQL server UTC clock; consuming at or after expiry denies. Missing/invalid TTL denies issue/use, not unrelated admission that needs no confirmation.

## Evolution and legacy provenance

This plan reserves no migration number and changes no published migration. At authorized implementation, inspect the actual immediate predecessor and compose minimal forward-only changes for operation scope, required projections/guards and only genuinely missing fields. Do not duplicate existing completion, queue, claim or review tables. Preserve tenant keys, IDs, histories, frozen amounts and authentic source ancestry on representative populated upgrade; unknown historical authorship remains unverified/fail-closed, never repaired by inventing an attestation or Doctor action.

Required fields above express accepted outcomes and proposed representation; executable grant/trigger/lock proof remains with the owners. A missing upstream representation/evidence is handled by the [prerequisite register](research.md#upstream-prerequisite-register), not a silent Wave 3 reassignment.
